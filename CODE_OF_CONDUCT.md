# Code of conduct

This project is a place to argue about design and security. Disagree with ideas as hard as you like; do not attack people.

Expected: be specific, assume good faith, credit others' work, keep criticism about the work.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing others' private information, or pushing exploit details of an unfixed vulnerability in public (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem with a maintainer's or a contributor's behaviour, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or open a private security advisory draft and say it is a conduct report.
